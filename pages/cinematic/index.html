FAQ

Questions and answers.

How long does a typical project take?

Most automations and AI agents ship in 2 to 6 weeks, depending on scope. A focused pilot can be live in days; larger, multi-system builds take longer. You get a clear timeline after the first call.

Do you work with my existing website and tools?

Yes. I connect to what you already use: WordPress, your CRM, email platform, sheets, WhatsApp and more. If you do not have a site yet, I can build that too.

What does it cost?

Fixed-price packages start at 299 euros and are listed on the pricing page. Larger builds are quoted per project within 48 hours. No surprise fees.

Will the automations keep running without you?

Yes. Everything is deployed, monitored and documented, then handed over to you. Optional ongoing support is available if you want me to keep an eye on it.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own tools and infrastructure wherever possible. I follow GDPR practices and can work within your security requirements.

Which languages and regions do you serve?

I work remotely across Europe and the Middle East, in English, German, French, Romanian and Arabic.
