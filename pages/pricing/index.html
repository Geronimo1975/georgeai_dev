No. Every package on this page is fixed-price and can be bought without speaking to anyone. A call is available if you want one, but it is not a gate you have to pass through — that is the whole point of putting real prices on a public page.

You get a Stripe receipt immediately, then an email from me within one business day with the two or three questions I need answered to start. For lead packs that is your target market; for systems it is access to whatever the automation has to talk to.

Can I cancel a monthly service?

Yes, with one month's notice, and the setup fee is never charged twice. Everything built for you stays yours — the workflows, the prompts and the configuration are handed over, not held hostage.

What happens to my data?

It stays in the EU and it stays yours. Processing runs on EU infrastructure under a data processing agreement (AV-Vertrag), no customer data is used to train models, and every AI assistant discloses that it is AI, as the EU AI Act requires.

Why is there no VAT on the invoice?

Because of the German small-business rule, § 19 UStG, no VAT is charged. The price you see is the price you pay — €299 is charged as €299, with nothing added at checkout. If you are a business buyer, note that this also means there is no input tax for you to reclaim.
